# Multiplicative Composition

A mathematical investigation into why independent dimensions compose multiplicatively - not additively - to produce emergent properties, and why this structure appears across unrelated domains.

Paper 1: **Axiomatic Characterization of Multiplicative Aggregation: On Zero-Collapse and the O-Ring Inversion** (papers/paper1/paper_v2.tex)

## The Claim

Given N orthogonal measures on a system, their multiplicative product captures synergistic properties that additive combination does not. If any factor is trivial, the product collapses. The composition has properties none of the components have alone.

This is not a metaphor. It is a specific mathematical structure that appears independently in:

- **Information theory** - Partial Information Decomposition (Williams & Beer, 2010): synergistic information exists only in the joint distribution
- **Economics** - Cobb-Douglas production functions: Y = A × K^α × L^β (complementary inputs, 1927)
- **Physics** - Tensor products: composite quantum systems exhibit entanglement absent in components
- **Complex systems** - Causal emergence (Hoel et al.): macro-level descriptions contain more causally relevant information
- **Consciousness science** - HIRM equation: C(t) = Φ(t) × R(t) × D(t) (the originating observation)

## Research Questions

1. Is there a general theorem characterizing when multiplicative composition of independent measures produces synergistic (in the PID sense) information?
2. What are the necessary and sufficient conditions on the component dimensions for the product to exhibit emergent properties?
3. Does the multiplicative structure predict empirically testable properties that additive composition does not?
4. Can we prove that systems exhibiting this structure are more robust, more information-rich, or more causally emergent than additive systems?

## Origin

This research originated from the HIRM (Hierarchical Information-Reality Model) equation for consciousness, where C = Φ × R × D. The multiplicative structure was initially a claim about subjective experience. Subsequent work across multiple domains (distributed AI orchestration, cybersecurity deception, network intelligence, dependency analysis) revealed the same structural pattern appearing independently - prompting the question of whether the equation describes something more general than consciousness.

## Status

Early-stage theoretical investigation. Literature survey in progress.
