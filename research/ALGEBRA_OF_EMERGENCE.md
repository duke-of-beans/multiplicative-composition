## 2. THE CORE THESIS

### Why Multiplication, Not Addition

When you write f(x1, x2, ..., xn) = ∏xi, you're making a specific claim: each dimension is individually necessary but only jointly sufficient. Deficiency on any single dimension is fatal to the whole. Zero on any axis annihilates everything else.

The opposite structure — addition (weighted sum, linear combination, averaging) — claims dimensions are substitutable. Weakness in one area can be compensated by strength in another.

**The central argument:** For emergent properties — consciousness, trustworthiness, information quality, source reliability, organizational effectiveness, systemic stability — substitutability is empirically wrong. Additive models systematically overestimate quality by hiding zeros inside averages. Multiplicative models surface the binding constraint.

### Why It Keeps Appearing

Emergent properties require multiple independent conditions to be simultaneously satisfied. If any condition is absent, the emergent property doesn't partially exist — it doesn't exist at all. This is AND-gated, not OR-gated. The mathematical structure that models co-occurrence of independent conditions is multiplication: P(A AND B) = P(A) × P(B) when independent.

The multiplicative structure isn't an empirical discovery that happens to fit. It's the necessary algebraic consequence of how independent dimensions interact when all are required.

### The Zero-Annihilation Property

In every domain: the most important diagnostic isn't the high scores — it's the zeros. ASURIQ: a model with zero dissent quality is a sycophant regardless of accuracy. Tranche: a source with zero corroboration is rumor regardless of reporter reliability. HIRM: a system with zero feedback has zero consciousness regardless of integration. IQF: information with zero grounding is speculation regardless of surprisal. Multiplication forces the system to surface the weakest dimension as the binding constraint. Additive models hide it.

### Connection to Existing Mathematics

Not coming from nowhere — connects to:
- Probability theory (independent events multiply)
- Information theory (independent sources compound information gain multiplicatively)
- Cobb-Douglas production function (non-substitutable factors)
- Series reliability in engineering (system reliability = ∏ component reliabilities)
- Fisher information geometry (natural metric decomposes multiplicatively along independent parameter axes)
- Condorcet Jury Theorem (independent voter accuracy)

The contribution: showing these are all instances of ONE structure, not independent discoveries.

## 3. DOMAIN EXAMPLES (PAPER-READY)

### 3.1 Mechanical Engineering

**Existing:** Series reliability is multiplicative. Engineers know this for reliability.

**Novel:** Design evaluation hasn't internalized it. The Pugh matrix is additive — score concepts on weighted criteria, sum. If safety, manufacturability, cost are non-substitutable, the additive score masks a concept brilliant on 9 dimensions and fatally flawed on the 10th. Gets 7/10 average. Multiplicative gives zero.

**Killer example:** Boeing 737 MAX MCAS. System zero (pilot training adequacy for MCAS behavior) averaged into overall design evaluation rather than treated as binding constraint. Additive said "acceptable." People died. Multiplicative would have surfaced the zero pre-certification.

**Testable claim:** Retrospective multiplicative scoring of pre-certification 737 MAX data would produce zero where additive models produced "acceptable." Falsifiable with public data.

### 3.2 Economies / Development

**Existing:** Cobb-Douglas is multiplicative. Development economics ignores structural implications.

**Novel:** 70 years of additive development thinking: build schools AND hospitals AND roads, sum investments, expect proportional outcomes. Multiplicative explains "big push" failures (Sachs/Easterly): zero institutional quality × infinite capital = zero development. Binding constraint (governance) must be addressed first.

**Explains:** Small number of successes (South Korea, Singapore, Botswana) all addressed ALL preconditions including institutional quality. Aid without governance reform = zero emergence.

**Connection:** David's essay "Such a Thing as Society" — intuitive recognition of what the multiplicative model formalizes.

### 3.3 Financial Systems / 2008

**Novel:** Systemic risk is multiplicative. When liquidity × counterparty × market × operational risk co-occur, total is the product, not sum.

**2008 as multiplicative failure modeled with additive tools:**
- Each individual risk "manageable" — additive models said so
- When counterparty trust hit ZERO, interbank lending ceased overnight
- Zero trust × infinite capital reserves = zero lending
- Fed injected unlimited liquidity — didn't matter until trust restored
- Additive predicted gradual degradation. Reality was zero-collapse.

**Regulatory implication:** Multiplicative systemic risk modeling changes diagnostic from "aggregate exposure?" to "which dimension approaches zero?" In 2006-07: counterparty transparency. Multiplicative framework surfaces this BEFORE collapse. Early warning is structural, not statistical. Basel and stress testing implications.

**Credit scoring:** FICO is additive. Perfect payment history + zero income: additive says 750, multiplicative says insolvent. Experienced lenders do multiplicative correction informally — checking for zeros the additive score hides.

**Testable:** Retrospective multiplicative model of 2007 inputs would predict collapse where additive predicted stability.

### 3.7 Religion — DUAL COMPOSITION ANALYSIS

**David's challenge:** Any container with same dimensional structure produces same benefits. Is the cost worth it? Religion = leading cause of death outside nature/disease.

**Novel framework — Dual Composition:**

Religion runs TWO multiplicative compositions simultaneously, producing TWO emergent properties:

**Composition 1 — Individual Wellbeing Emergence:**
shared narrative × communal practice × moral framework × mutual care × transcendence/meaning

**Composition 2 — Institutional Power Emergence:**
hierarchy × in-group/out-group boundary enforcement × unfalsifiable authority × perpetuation mechanisms (childhood indoctrination, apostasy penalties) × resource extraction

Religion's advantage: maximizes BOTH. Not better at individual wellbeing — better at institutional power. Power determines which containers survive at civilizational scale.

**Displacement mechanism:** Indigenous practices, mystery schools, contemplative traditions had Composition 1 saturated. Produced individual emergence. Lacked Composition 2. Religion (maximizing both) systematically destroyed containers maximizing only Composition 1. Not better for individuals — better at institutional violence.

**Reframed trolley problem:** Not "are benefits worth costs?" They're outputs of DIFFERENT compositions. Question: can you saturate Composition 1 without activating Composition 2? Model says yes — different dimensional sets. You don't need hierarchy for community, unfalsifiable authority for shared narrative, boundary enforcement for mutual care.

**Scale problem:** Nobody has done this at civilizational scale. Containers stripping Composition 2 stay small. Composition 2 produces scale and persistence. Hierarchy enables coordination across millions. Perpetuation ensures generational continuity. Unfalsifiable authority prevents schism.

**The open question:** Is there a third composition producing civilizational-scale coordination WITHOUT hierarchy, unfalsifiable authority, and boundary enforcement? If not, Composition 1-only containers will always be small, marginal, and vulnerable. Finding it might be the most important design problem in human history.

**Unfalsifiable authority as critical dimension:** The dimension that makes religion uniquely destructive vs merely tribalistic. Sports create in-group/out-group. Nations create hierarchy. Parties create perpetuation. None claim authority beyond human verification. You can argue with a coach, president, party leader. Can't argue with God. Unfalsifiability removes feedback loop constraining all other institutional power. Turns hierarchy into tyranny, boundary enforcement into crusade, perpetuation into inquisition.

**Wellbeing data nuance:** Social desirability bias documented. But strongest evidence = objective measures: mortality (VanderWeele, JAMA — 26-33% lower among regular attenders), cortisol, immune markers, blood pressure, telomere length. Can't fake. Model handles both: community-level emergence doesn't guarantee individual-level. Individual with zero personal meaning (going through motions) has binding constraint. Emergence operates at every level.

**Psychological exploitation (David's point):** Manipulation degrades transcendence (meaning → fear/obligation). Institutional self-interest degrades pastoral care (care → control). Dimensions degraded toward zero within system. Model predicts: emergence collapses even though structure persists. Institution survives. Emergence doesn't. Form of religion without function.

## 4. META-IMPLICATIONS (DOMAIN-GENERAL)

### 4.1 The Binding Constraint Principle
In any system with non-substitutable dimensions, the most important question is never "what's our average?" It's "where is our zero?" All investment targets binding constraint first — improvement elsewhere has zero marginal return.

### 4.2 The Additive Audit
Every scoring system can be audited: does it add when it should multiply? If dimensions are non-substitutable preconditions for emergence, it's producing systematically overconfident scores. Empirically testable.

### 4.3 The Emergence Threshold
When all dimensions above threshold, emergence appears. When any drops below, emergence doesn't degrade proportionally — it vanishes. Why complex systems exhibit nonlinear collapse. Multiplicative predicts this; additive can't.

### 4.4 The Diagnostic Criterion
If dimensions are genuinely independent and individually necessary, multiplicative composition isn't a choice — it's the only structure preserving the logic.

### 8.1 LUCE 1965 — FULL PAPER READ, VERDICT: DOES NOT SUBSUME

**Paper:** Luce, R.D. (1965). "A 'Fundamental' Axiomatization of Multiplicative Power Relations Among Three Variables." Philosophy of Science 32(3/4): 301-309.

**Read in full** via JSTOR (David created account, uploaded all 9 pages as images, Claude read them).

**Luce's problem:** When two independent measurement systems exist for physical quantities (conjoint measurement via trade-offs and extensive measurement via concatenation), under what conditions are the conjoint scales power functions of the extensive scales?

**David's problem:** What is the unique valid composition function for emergent properties from N independent dimensions?

**Key differences:**
1. **Concatenation required (Luce) vs not (David).** Luce's Axioms E1-E6 require physical concatenation operations (placing masses together, adding velocities). David's framework requires no concatenation — critical because you can't "concatenate" institutional trust or ecosystem resilience. Luce's framework CANNOT be applied to David's domains.
2. **Zero-collapse absent from Luce.** Luce works on positive reals. Zero isn't in the domain. The zero-annihilation property — cliffs, binding constraints, the entire policy apparatus — has no analog in Luce.
3. **Three variables (Luce) vs N dimensions (David).** Luce proves for two components + one derived quantity. David proves for arbitrary N. The generalization isn't trivial.
4. **Shared backbone:** Both use the multiplicative Cauchy functional equation f(rs) = f(r)f(s) → f(r) = rᵧ. This is standard from Aczél (1966), not proprietary to either.

**How to cite:** "Luce (1965) proved that conjoint measurement scales are power functions of extensive measurement scales for physical quantities with concatenation operations. The present work addresses a different question — the unique form of the composition function for emergent properties — and differs in three structural ways: it requires no concatenation operations on the component dimensions, it includes zero-collapse as a defining axiom, and it generalizes to N dimensions."

**The EXPLORATION engine's DISCOVER mode (Claude) overstated** the subsumption claim. Luce does not subsume David's proof. Different problem, different axioms, different scope. Shared mathematical backbone ≠ subsumption.
